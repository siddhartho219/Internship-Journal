# Privacy Rules — Public Repo

Never write into any journal entry, summary, or commit:
- Company name, client names, or names of internal products/services
- Internal URLs, internal repo links, ticket/issue links
- API keys, tokens, passwords, connection strings
- Screenshots or file paths that reveal internal systems
- Exact business metrics that could identify the company (revenue,
  exact customer counts, unreleased feature names)

When in doubt, generalize:
"the client's system" not the client's name.
"an internal API" not the actual endpoint.
"a production issue" not the specific service name.
