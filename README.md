# Internship Journal

A running log of what I worked on and learned during my internship,
turned from quick daily notes into readable entries with the help of Claude.

**Note:** Company names, client names, and internal details are intentionally
generalized or omitted throughout this repo. Entries reflect my own
experience and learning, not confidential work product.

- `journal/` — daily entries, grouped by month
- `summaries/` — monthly and quarterly highlights, resume-ready bullets
- `templates/` — the format used for entries and summaries
